THE LONG-HORIZON LOOP

Facts first. Context next. Advice last.

A daily plan cannot be published from one model response. MealCircuit separates evidence, case understanding, professional limits, strategy, planning, and independent review before you accept anything.

  1. 01 RECORD

    Capture reality

    Record meals, photos, ingredients, hunger, sleep, training, symptoms, and corrections without forcing missing details into fake precision.

  2. 02 UNDERSTAND

    Read the person

    Bring current goals, personal doctrine, recent patterns, daily constraints, and confirmed preferences into the same decision context.

  3. 03 DESIGN

    Compare real choices

    Balance safety, portions, recovery, meal mode, preparation time, and ingredient reuse before designing a reviewed next-day draft.

  4. 04 CALIBRATE

    Learn from outcomes

    Keep corrections and execution feedback as history. Promote recurring needs only through visible, reversible, evidence-backed learning.

SOVEREIGNTY BY DEFAULT

Your history stays useful without becoming someone else’s cloud.

The local database is the source of truth. Sync and model calls are separate choices, with explicit boundaries for what leaves the device.

THREE EVERYDAY SURFACES

The machinery stays behind the decision.

Ordinary navigation focuses on what matters now: the day, the plan, and the personal context shaping it.

01 · TODAY

Notice change.

See today’s arrangement, record what shifted, answer only relevant questions, and negotiate tomorrow’s draft.

02 · PLANS

03 · ME

Own the rules.

Manage goals, meal modes, safety boundaries, inventory, learned preferences, appearance, and advanced settings.

THE HONEST BOUNDARY

Useful support, without invented certainty.

MealCircuit is designed to make uncertainty visible. Safety and user confirmation are part of the product, not footnotes after generation.

×

No diagnosis

It provides general logging and decision support, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

×

No invisible facts

Oil, sauces, weights, brands, health states, and inventory stay unknown until evidence supports them.

×

No official hosted cloud

Accounts exist only for optional compatible self-hosted synchronization.

×

No silent publishing

A reviewed draft remains replaceable until the user explicitly accepts it as the formal plan.
